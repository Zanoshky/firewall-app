When something goes wrong

What you see, why it happens, and the taps that fix it. More answers are in the questions.

An app or a site stopped working

Something it needs was stopped: the app itself, a tracker list, or one of your rules.

  1. Open Activity and find its lookups marked as stopped.
  2. Tap one and pick Always allow, or Allow for a blocked app.
  3. Still broken? Set the app to Not filtered on the Apps tab, then close and reopen it.

Firewall turned itself off

Android allows one VPN at a time, so another VPN app stops Firewall. Some phones also stop apps in the background to save battery.

  1. Switch it back on from Home or the Quick Settings tile.
  2. Android settings, Apps, Firewall, Battery: pick Unrestricted.
  3. Network and internet, VPN, Firewall: turn on Always on VPN.

Nothing shows in Activity

With Private DNS set to a provider, Android sends lookups straight there and Firewall never sees them.

  1. Tap the Private DNS warning on Home.
  2. Let Firewall switch it to Automatic while it runs, or set it yourself under Network and internet.

A blocked app still gets online

It was already connected, or it connects straight to an address without a lookup.

  1. Close the app and open it again.
  2. With Pro, turn on the Kill switch (Android 13 or newer, with Shizuku).

The Kill switch stopped after a restart

Shizuku has to be started again after every restart; Firewall reminds you.

  1. Open Shizuku and tap Start.

Until then, Blocked works the normal way.

Data used or a cap shows nothing

Firewall reads Android's own data counts, which need Usage access.

  1. Open the app's page and tap Allow usage access.
  2. Turn Firewall on in Android's list.

A tracker list will not download

The list's server is slow or gone, or the phone is short on memory for very big lists.

  1. Open Settings, Tracker lists, and tap Refresh on that list.
  2. Still failing? Remove one of the biggest lists; the page shows the memory each takes.

Forgot the App Lock passcode

The passcode is kept only on the phone, and nobody, us included, can read it.

  1. Android settings, Apps, Firewall, Storage, Clear storage.
  2. Open Firewall and restore your last backup.

Pro is missing on a new phone

  1. Sign in to Google Play with the account that bought it.
  2. Open Firewall. Pro comes back on its own.

The subscription will not start

Support And Live API runs through the Google Play app. A copy not installed from Google Play, an old Play Store, or no account on the phone cannot reach it.

  1. Open the Play Store once and check you are signed in.
  2. Update Firewall and the Play Store.
  3. Try the row again in Settings, under Support the developer.

To cancel: tap the same row, or Play Store, your profile, Payments and subscriptions.
