Cookbook

Short recipes

A few taps each. Every recipe starts from the bar at the bottom: Home, Apps, Activity and Settings.

Stop trackers, keep the app working

  1. Open Apps and tap the app.
  2. Pick Filtered.
  3. Close the app and open it again.

It works as before, and Activity shows each tracker it tried to reach.

Keep an app off mobile data

  1. Open Apps and tap the app.
  2. Under Can connect on, tap Mobile data to block it there.

The list then says Wi-Fi only for that app. Roaming counts as mobile data too.

Block a site in every app

  1. Open Settings, Your domain rules.
  2. Add the domain to the block list, like example.com.

It covers www.example.com and every other name under it, for every app set to Filtered.

Safe search for a child's phone

  1. Open Settings, Encrypted lookups.
  2. Turn on Forced SafeSearch.
  3. In the browser, turn off its own Secure DNS.
  4. Turn on App Lock in Settings, so nobody changes it.

Quiet nights

  1. Open Settings, Screen off.
  2. Turn on Block while the screen is off.
  3. Under Stay online, add the apps you want to hear from, like your messenger.

It starts a minute after the screen goes off, and calls, messages and Google Play services keep working.

A daily mobile data cap (Pro)

  1. Open Apps and tap the app.
  2. Tap Daily mobile data cap and pick an amount.
  3. Allow Usage access when Firewall asks.

Cut an app off completely (Pro)

  1. Set the app to Blocked.
  2. Open Settings, Kill switch, and follow the steps: get Shizuku, start it, allow Firewall.

Needs Android 13 or newer. After a restart, open Shizuku and tap Start once.

Your rules on a new phone

  1. On the old phone, Settings, Backup and restore, save a backup.
  2. Copy the file to the new phone.
  3. On the new phone, Backup and restore, Restore from file.

Your passcode and your activity are never in the backup.

Support Firewall monthly

  1. Open Settings and scroll to Support the developer.
  2. Tap Support And Live API.
  3. Google Play shows the price and how to cancel. Confirm there.

Optional, and it unlocks nothing. Tap the row again to manage or cancel it.
